Every voice
has a story. Let us hear yours.

Vocalize is a personal vocal coach that listens the way a great teacher does. Sing a few bars and it follows every note, measuring your pitch to the cent, the breath beneath each phrase, the shimmer of your vibrato and the color of your tone. Then it tells you, kindly and clearly, how to sing even better.

The Vocalize philosophy
The method

A practice in three movements

I

Sing

Allegro

Record in your browser or bring a recording. Ten to thirty seconds of any song you love is enough. No studio, no special microphone.

II

Listen

Andante

More than twenty careful measurements unfold together: where each note lands, how your breath carries a phrase, and how you move between chest, mix and head voice.

III

Bloom

Crescendo

Receive the one thing to work on first, exercises chosen for your voice type, and a quiet record of every session so you can hear yourself grow.

Tonight's program

Everything a teacher would hear

Intonationto the cent

Note-by-note accuracy, whether you drift sharp or flat, and the exact notes to practice.

Vibratorate & width

Its speed, width and evenness, compared with the ideal for your voice.

Breathsupport & phrasing

How long each phrase lasts, how steady your air pressure is, and where breathiness creeps in.

Resonancering & color

The singer's ring that lets a voice carry, along with brightness, warmth and nasality.

Registerschest, mix, head

Every register you sing in, and how smoothly you cross the passaggio.

Dynamicspianissimo to forte

How well you control volume, and whether your phrases rise and fall with intention.

Voice typesoprano to bass

Your natural range, identified so every tip is tuned to the voice you actually have.

Onsetsthe first moment

How each note begins, whether clean, breathy or scooped, and how it ends.
